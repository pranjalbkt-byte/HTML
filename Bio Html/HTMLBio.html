Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a great Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He is popularly known as the Missile Man of India.

Early Life

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams.

Important Achievement

One of his greatest achievements was his contribution to India's missile and space programmes. He played an important role in developing India's defence technology.

He played a key role in India's missile development programme.

He always believed in the power of dreams and hard work.

His most admirable quality was his dedication to the nation.

He received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, in 1997.
